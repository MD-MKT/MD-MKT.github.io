Platform: Instagram, website, paid trafficGoal: Clarity, consistency and sold-out group programs for a solo expertWhen: 2023 to 2025

The big idea

Luciane was running her own marketing alone and burning out. We gave her a clear voice, a premium presence and paid traffic, so she could show up consistently and sell out her programs.

The real problem

Overwhelmed managing marketing solo, with a message that lacked clarity and an audience that was not growing.
