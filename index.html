About

Two years inside one creator's business taught me what a system has to survive.

From October 2023 to 2025 I worked inside business mentor Eduardo Araujo's The 1% Academy, from Senior Video Editor to Head of Content and Marketing Manager, running content, launches and marketing operations as his right hand. In 2023 I co-founded OASIS Creative Co with my partner, where I lead growth strategy, paid acquisition, funnels, CRO and lifecycle automation and she leads creative direction and brand.
